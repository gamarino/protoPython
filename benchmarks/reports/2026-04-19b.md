**Change:** Step 2 — shard `mutableRoot` × 16 in protoCore.
`ProtoSpace::mutableRoot` is now `std::atomic<ProtoSparseList*> mutableRoot[16]`.

from 16 concurrent writers to ~1 per shard in the worst case.
