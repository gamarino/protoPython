**Notes:**
- `call_recursion` (fib(25)) timed out at 90s — CPU powersave throttling artifact.
- Clearest Step 3 signal: `list_append_loop` 47.98x → 36.29x (**~25% improvement**). This workload
  creates one new AVL node per append (100k allocations), so eliminating 63/64 STW atomic loads
  shows directly here.
- `multithread_cpu` 96.31x → 82.89x (**~14% improvement**) — confirms batched STW poll also helps
  multi-threaded workloads competing for per-allocation checks.
- `str_concat_loop` and `attr_lookup` show slight fluctuation in this 2-run quick mode; these are
  within measurement noise (~10%).
- Geomean: 36.65x (Step 2) → 35.76x (Step 3): **~2.4% additional improvement** at geomean level.
  The headroom is smaller here because heavier benchmarks (memory_pressure, str_concat) are
  dominated by other costs.
- Cumulative improvement from V92 baseline: **56.4x → 35.76x (~37% total)**.

**Change:** Step 3 — batched STW poll in `ProtoContext::allocCell()`.
Replaced unconditional `stwFlag.load()` (seq_cst atomic per call) with a poll gated on
`(allocatedCellsCount & 63) == 0`. Uses the existing `allocatedCellsCount` field — no new
struct members, no ABI changes. The cooperative GC can tolerate a ~3µs delay (64 allocations
at ~50ns each) before a thread parks; the `implSynchToGC()` path in `implAllocCell` already
handles hard STW checks when the per-thread cell batch is exhausted.
